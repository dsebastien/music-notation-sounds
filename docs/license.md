# License

This package holds two kinds of things, under two licenses. The full text of both is in [`LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/dsebastien/music-notation-sounds/blob/main/LICENSE.md).

## The sound files: the GeneralUser GS license

The soundfonts in `generaluser/` are cut from GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins. They are under the GeneralUser GS license, version 2.0, in [`generaluser/LICENSE.txt`](https://github.com/dsebastien/music-notation-sounds/blob/main/generaluser/LICENSE.txt).

What it says, in short:

- You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own music, private or commercial.
- You may use it in your software projects, and change the bank or its packaging to suit your needs. That's what this package does: it cuts the bank into one file per instrument, compresses the samples and gives each file a gain.
- The samples allow full use in music production, including making money from recordings made with them.
- The author can't be 100% sure where every sample came from. The author knows none came from commercial soundfont packages or sample CDs, and has never received a complaint about sample ownership since 2000. Still, this may concern you if you ship the files in a commercial software product: read the license before you do.
- If you feature GeneralUser GS on your own website, link to [the author's website](https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser), not to the download files.
